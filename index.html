Design-to-Code and Human-AI Collaboration · Warp

Personal project · 2026 · Angular, Knowledge Architecture, AI Agents

Role
Design & research
Year
2026
Status
Public
Output
Methodology + repositories

Research into how much of the definition → design/prototyping → frontend implementation flow can be delegated to AI agents, and under what conditions this can be done reliably, traceably and reproducibly. Includes real design-to-code conversion tests on Angular, with agents that had no prior conversation history, to evaluate replicability and context dependency.

I developed a staged methodology based on traceability, ambiguity reduction, cumulative enrichment, versioning and validation gates, with explicit contracts between layers; the tests identified the design-to-code specification contract as the main bottleneck. Splitting research, documentation, testing and development across multiple agents surfaced a second problem: keeping knowledge, decisions and responsibilities coherent across instances with no shared memory.

In response I built Warp, a Git-based knowledge architecture for maintaining persistent responsibilities, interchangeable agents, explicit documentary authority and automatic structural validation, critically contrasted against DDD / Bounded Contexts, Data Mesh, ADR/RFC, IBIS, W3C PROV and Team Topologies.
